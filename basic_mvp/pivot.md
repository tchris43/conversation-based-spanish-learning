# Pivot

We are trying to build a roadmap for someone to truly be able to learn Spanish according to their goals, and starting where they already are.

To do that:

1. First, we need to ask them their goals.
2. Then, we need to come up with the right context to get a sense of where they stand in comparison to their goal.
3. Then, we need to come up with the right criteria to truly evaluate and know where they stand so we know:
   - their floor (where they start)
   - what they need to reach their goal
